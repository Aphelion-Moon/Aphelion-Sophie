    Spending
      

      

      ISO date and time with timezone. Leave empty to keep paid calls paused.
      

      

      How spending is counted
      Calendar months use Europe/Vienna. There is no separate daily money cap. The technical limit is 2,000 attempts per day. At 80% of the money ceiling, optional proactive calls stop first.
      Reservations use conservative estimates. Unknown charges remain reserved. These figures are not a reconciled provider invoice or an exact currency guarantee. An unreviewed or expired price pauses paid calls.
      Resolved attempt details expire after 90 days; daily totals after 90 days and monthly totals after 400 days once unreferenced. Unresolved charges and replay receipts remain.
      

      

      Clear an accounting hold

      First reconcile every pending attempt and record review of the price and reservation estimate that caused the overrun. Clearing this hold keeps all charges and budget limits.
