How processing works

When you opt in, eligible messages you send in that channel may be processed by the local model and included in bounded temporary context. Its configured mode determines whether Sophie speaks, answers questions, reacts or waits to be addressed. Replies and reactions are visible to that channel’s audience. Discord keeps its own message history.

Personal chat memory, model training, automatic learning and cloud processing are off. Your channel choice and consent revision are retained to enforce opt-outs. Contact the human operators for data access or deletion requests. Opting out stops new processing and invalidates pending work; it cannot retract already processed text or delivered Discord messages. Host operators can administer the system.

Do not submit private cases, onboarding content, sensitive information or another person’s private material. Those sources are excluded from Sophie’s AI. Use the ordinary human Staff contact route for questions or incident reports; AI does not read those reports.
