    What Sophie remembers

    When the processing notice says so, Sophie keeps short notes of her own from conversations she takes part in: things you tell her about yourself, and channel moments that mention you. Each is kept for 90 days after she last uses it and deleted when you leave, unless you chose to keep your AI data.

      How Sophie’s memories work

      Sophie writes a memory only in channels where you opted in, and only about members taking part in that conversation. She refers to members by an internal label, never by name, and contact details, links and account identifiers are masked before anything is kept. She may still get things wrong: edit or forget anything here. A memory about a channel moment that mentions you is deleted for everyone when you forget it.

      Turning memory off stops Sophie keeping or using anything new about you; forget the existing memories separately. Host operators can review and delete memories. Database backups follow their own retention, and a restored backup cannot bring back memories you deleted.

      If you leave the server

      By default, leaving the server deletes your saved AI preferences, the memories Sophie kept that mention you, channel consent records and personal AI activity records. Returning requires a fresh channel opt-in.

      What is kept and what is deleted

      Temporary context is cleared and processing stops even if you choose retention. Existing expiry limits still apply to retained data.

      Deletion is retried after outages. In-flight delivery and accounting records remain held until their outcome is resolved; minimal deletion and replay markers prevent restoration or resending. Backup copies and already transmitted requests follow their own retention. This setting applies to your AI data, not human case records.
