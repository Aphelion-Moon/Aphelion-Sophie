Tell Sophie about you

Up to eight short notes about yourself, one per line, kept for 90 days. Sophie uses them while your choice is Yes. Write only about yourself, and leave out sensitive details or anything about another person: Sophie replies in shared channels, so others may see a reply that uses a note.

What Sophie remembers

While your choice is Yes, Sophie may keep short notes of her own from conversations: things you tell her, and channel moments that mention you. Each lasts 90 days after she last uses it. Edit or forget any of them here.

    What deleting does

    Deleting removes your notes, every memory Sophie kept that mentions you, your choices and your personal AI activity records, and Sophie stops talking with you until you say yes again. Sophie’s replies that used your messages are removed from Discord. Requests already sent to the AI provider cannot be recalled, and backups follow their own retention; a restored backup cannot bring back what you deleted.
