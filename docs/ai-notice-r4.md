Sophie AI notice (revision 4)

What this is
Sophie is the Aphelion community bot. If you say yes, she can read your messages and reply using an AI model in the channels where she takes part. One choice covers the whole server. Saying no, or changing your mind later, does not affect your access to the community.

What is sent, and to whom
When Sophie answers, your recent messages in that channel, other members' recent messages there and her own replies go to Mistral AI (a company based in France) through its EU service. At most 12 recent messages are used, none older than 5 minutes. Common identifiers such as mentions, email addresses and phone numbers are removed first, but names and other details can slip through, so please do not share personal or sensitive information, or anything about another person. In help channels, words from a question about code may also be sent to GitHub's code search to find the right file.

Where Sophie never looks
Tickets, Staff contacts, onboarding (Shuttle) channels, their threads and attachments are always excluded. Private channels are excluded too, except staff channels the server owner has named; there, the same choice applies to each staff member.

What you tell Sophie
With /sophie you can give her up to eight short notes about yourself, kept for 90 days. She uses them while your choice is yes. Her replies are posted in shared channels, so others may see a reply that draws on a note.

What Sophie remembers
While your choice is yes, Sophie may keep short notes of her own from conversations: things you tell her, and channel moments that mention you. Each lasts 90 days after she last uses it. Memories from staff channels are used only in staff channels. You can see, edit and forget them at any time.

What is kept, and for how long
Sophie keeps your choice and the notice you accepted. If you leave the server, your notes, her memories of you, your choices and your personal AI activity records are deleted, unless you choose to keep them.

What Mistral AI keeps
Mistral AI processes requests as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. Our account has opted out of having its data used to train Mistral's models. Unless zero data retention is enabled for our account, Mistral may keep requests and replies for a limited time to detect abuse. Processing runs in the European Economic Area; any transfer outside it relies on the safeguards in Mistral's Data Processing Addendum.

Your choices and rights
Type /sophie at any time to say yes or no, get a copy of everything Sophie keeps about you, make her forget her memories, or delete your data. Saying no stops new processing and removes her replies that used your messages; it cannot recall a request already sent. For anything else, contact the server owners through the normal Staff contact route. Your consent does not waive any of your legal rights.
