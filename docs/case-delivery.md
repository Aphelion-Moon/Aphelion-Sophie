# Private case channel provisioning

Scope: the channel/provisioning portions of P06, P09–P11 and P19; partial evidence for T01, T07, T08, T18, T22, T36, T56, T57 and T59. These are local implementation checks, not live acceptance. There are no new dependencies, Discord credentials, case messages, forms, attachments or AI paths in this slice.

## Ownership and admission

`modules/tickets/channel-policy.js` defines bounded, versioned channel policy and exact audiences. Core injects verified guild, bot, category, Staff and lead ops IDs plus an explicit attachment-permission choice. No ID is resolved from a name. `apps/core/storage/case-store.js` owns reservations, immutable policy versions, provisioning state and channel exclusions. The shared membership operation preserves observed revocations even when a subsequent action is denied. Narrow channel observation proofs come from `apps/core/discord/case-channels.js`; serialized objects and caller-supplied channel IDs cannot confirm provisioning.

Reservations still require current self-service authorization, current guild presence, an idempotent interaction receipt and atomic member/guild capacity checks. A separate `presenceEpoch` advances on departure or an unprovable Gateway continuity gap, as described in [continuity checks](gateway-continuity.md). A request belongs to that provable membership episode: leaving and rejoining cannot revive it. Whitelist loss advances Shuttle eligibility independently and does not invalidate ordinary support requests. Existing moderation and BYOND role ownership are unchanged. [Signed Shuttle entry](shuttle-entry.md) shares these reservation helpers and binds a session to its private case atomically; [stage rendering](shuttle-screens.md) independently verifies current case permissions before delivery.

## Delivery and confirmation

1. Commit the reservation, policy version, random operation token and provisioning intent together.
2. Check current membership, bot identity, required permissions, category, activation and the durable delivery barrier. Commit `create_started` before attempting a channel create.
3. Create a text channel with a non-identifying name/marker and access restricted to the bot. Discord administrators remain a platform exception. Record every observed candidate and its permanent exclusion before opening access.
4. Fetch current metadata again. Set exactly the requester, configured responders and currently bound [explicit participants](case-participants.md) as the audience. Head Admin contact includes lead ops as responders; Staff Report includes Staff and lead ops. Staff requesters retain their own requester access. A reported subject is never automatically added.
5. Fetch the channel and member again. Verify exact overwrites, guild, category, marker, policy version and membership episode before marking the case open. The store independently compares the observed permissions against its policy, even if a misconfigured adapter reports success.

The adapter uses fixed [create-channel](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild#create-guild-channel) and [modify-channel](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/channel#modify-channel) routes. The latter sends only `parent_id` and `permission_overwrites`. It requires both Manage Channels and Manage Roles; the bot must hold the permissions it sets. It does not request Administrator. The pure evaluator implements Discord's [overwrite precedence and administrator bypass](https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/permissions#permission-overwrites). Role position cannot make a deny overwrite outrank an allow, and this evaluator is not a complete model of every Discord implicit permission or timeout rule.

Attachment access defaults to disabled only in the synthetic fixture. Production policy is unset. A permission allowing attachments does not establish file capture, safe storage or retention; that work and the owner's attachment limits remain required before enabling it.

## Lost responses, duplicates and late effects

The dispatcher attempts at most one external mutation per call. It never repeats a channel POST after creation may have started. A lost response is reconciled through the complete random marker and durable intent; a marker alone grants no access. A known channel that disappeared or changed marker is not recreated. If creation is uncertain and no result can be observed, the job parks and retains its capacity reservation. Even a pre-call interruption after committing intent requires reconciliation; this conservative choice prevents duplicate channels at the cost of operator intervention.

Every matching candidate is permanently registered, including duplicate channels and results returned to an expired worker. Unresolved candidates are sealed before parking. Removing one duplicate from Discord does not erase the recorded ambiguity. [Staff channel selection](shuttle-channel-selection.md) now records an explicit choice for bound Shuttle cases; every other retained candidate must be freshly verified bot-only before the chosen case can open. A previously opened case keeps its original destination. New candidates hold access pending another review. An expired worker can record a discovered channel but cannot acknowledge another worker's claim.

Departure during provisioning makes the desired channel bot-only. If it occurs while permissions are being changed, the next operation seals the channel before settling the failed request. Uncertain or late audience changes record a separate inspection job, even after a newer worker has finished. This repairs a late opening that arrives after an earlier seal. Repeated jobs converge on current stored policy and exact observed permissions. Failures retain channels, records and exclusions; no deletion route exists.

There is no transaction across PostgreSQL and Discord. An in-flight request can take effect after its lease expires, and transient exposure cannot be recalled. These checks do not prove uninterrupted ACL correctness during disconnects or after an external change to an already-open channel. The metadata journal now requests case inspection for relevant events and binds opaque case proofs to continuity versions. Its socket supervisor has local wire coverage; [periodic case inspections](case-inspections.md) now have offline coverage; live service composition remains required. Mapping changes must stop delivery and reconcile pending work; simply bumping a policy version parks older jobs and is not a complete permission migration.

## Evidence and remaining work

Six pure/adapter tests cover audience separation, Discord overwrite precedence, administrator bypass, attachment-deny behavior, strict policy matching, opaque/expiring contexts, token identity, category and bot permissions. C01–C21 exercise actual isolated PostgreSQL with simulated Discord HTTP, covering normal provisioning, Head Admin privacy, ambiguous creation, duplicate candidates, lease loss, departure/rejoin, Whitelist-only loss, moved/broadened channels, forged proofs, configuration mismatch, lost replies, late writes and delivery barriers. S13–S14 retain atomic quota, authorization and permanent-exclusion checks through the concrete dispatcher.

[Staff delivery review](shuttle-artifact-recovery.md) exposes audited rechecks for parked provisioning jobs bound to Shuttle cases. F01–F03 verify that a late marked candidate is inspected without another POST, an absent result remains parked, and a departed member's late result is sealed. Q01–Q18 and S23 add explicit duplicate selection, retained inventories, sealing, late-write compensation and restart. General repair routes for other case types remain open.

[Versioned case lifecycle](case-lifecycle.md) now supplies signed Staff status/close/reopen commands, retained action audits, read-only closure, worker-confirmed permissions and guarded reopening. Closing immediately invalidates pending Shuttle grants and retires controls; reopening alone cannot grant Whitelist or clear a pause. The existing-audience read-only policy is a provisional offline default awaiting the owner's audience decision. [Staff case queues and assignment](case-staff.md) use current responder permissions and the same resource version. [Ordinary ticket intake](case-intake.md) now supplies Quick Help and versioned modal submissions. [Answer delivery](case-intake-delivery.md) and [explicit participants](case-participants.md) are now implemented. [Staff-contact intake and recipient navigation](case-contacts.md) now reuse these checks. Approved form copy, staff notes, transcript/file capture, confidential retrieval, exceptional deletion and general operator repair remain open. All channels remain present; category capacity and an archive strategy need implementation before sustained use. Discord documents a maximum of 50 children per category in its [channel structure](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/channel#channel-object).

Migration `005-case-provisioning.sql` adds immutable case policies, operation metadata, the retained channel registry and departure epochs. Existing membership rows acquire epoch zero; old case reservations deliberately do not receive invented policy/operation provenance and fail closed. Core needs SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on the new tables, with no DELETE. Migrations 001–004 remain unchanged. Only new synthetic clusters were migrated; none of the host's existing databases or services was changed.

Rollback: stop delivery, preserve all database/channel state and exclusions, and restore compatible application code. Do not drop the new tables or restart older code against their changed membership contract. No production deployment or down migration was performed. Production activation remains disabled until live mappings/permissions, trusted observers, missing case services, Windows identity boundaries and independent recovery gates are satisfied.
